# Dropsworth video style guide

Every shot type always means the same thing. Pick b-roll by the meaning of the line.

## Visual language (explainer / Nathan-style)

| Shot | Means | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Tired / stressed guy at laptop | struggling, losing money | hook / problem |
| Guy sitting at computer | working, doing the research | "they validate", effort beats |
| Shopify sales notifications on a phone | sales = dopamine = money | "it works", "scale it" |
| Real viral dropshipping product (gadgets, galaxy projector...) | the product itself, TikTok hype | when talking about products |
| Flashy supercar (bright, daytime / dusk) | winners, success | "the ones who win" |
| Bright penthouse / guy in penthouse | made it | **end of the video** |
| Cash | money (sparingly) | budget beats |

## Rules
- **The first shot always has a PERSON in it** so it feels relatable: a guy with a supercar, a guy in a penthouse, or a guy at a computer. Never open on an empty car, empty room, product or graphic.
- **Mostly real video.** Graphics only where footage can't show it (store count, scorecard, comment CTA). Never open on a graphic.
- **A guy** as the main character in people shots.
- **No box character** in explainers.
- **Bright, punchy grade** for explainers (`grade=bright`). **Dark moody grade** only for sauce posts.
- **Sound effects almost none** (soft whoosh on swipes only).
- No clips with burned-in text, watermarks, or AI look.
- **Never use McLaren clips** (owner's rule). Other supercars are fine.

## Sauce posts
- One moving dark aesthetic clip (night penthouse, supercar), small TikTok Sans text in one low-key clump, no dashes, nonchalant.
- Silent; sound added in the TikTok app (calm piano / luxury lofi).
