# Dropsworth brief (read by the nightly script writer)

## What Dropsworth is
A tool that **validates dropshipping products before you spend money on them**: it checks demand, margin and competition so beginners stop burning ad budgets on products that were never going to work.

## Audience
Beginner and early dropshippers (mostly 17–30). They watch TikTok "how I made $X" content, they've usually lost money on 1–10 products, they think "dropshipping is dead" or that they just need a better product. They respond to: confident, fast, no-fluff advice; money; the lifestyle (cars, penthouses); feeling understood ("I was there too").

## Voice and tone
- Talks TO the viewer ("you"), like a friend who figured it out. Confident, a bit blunt, never preachy or corporate.
- Fast. Short sentences. No filler words, no "in this video", no "hey guys".
- Real, specific, usable advice. Never vague motivation.
- No fake income claims and no made-up statistics. Don't say "I made $50k". Opinions and rules of thumb are fine ("5 to 15 stores is the sweet spot").
- No em dashes or en dashes in any on-screen text.

## Call to action (end of EVERY voiced video)
"Send me the word VALIDATE and I'll send you my free checklist."
(Video ends on a DM graphic showing the word being sent.)

## Things the owner rejected (never do these)
- Dark, gloomy explainers (dark grade is ONLY for sauce posts)
- Opening on a graphic, an empty car, an empty room or a product (first shot = a PERSON)
- Generic stick figures / box mascot in explainers
- Too many sound effects
- Graphics that look like an "AI explainer" (keep graphics to where footage can't show it)
- Long walls of text, dashes, or "teacher" tone in sauce posts
- "Comment VALIDATE" (old CTA) — it's now "send me the word VALIDATE"

## Approved examples (copy their pacing)

- `scripts/f4-steps.spec.json` — 5 steps to a winning product, supercars between steps (~54s)
- `scripts/f2-sauce.props.json` — sauce post, small nonchalant text over a dark penthouse clip
